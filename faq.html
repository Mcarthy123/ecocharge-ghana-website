Frequently Asked Questions

Everything drivers and partners commonly ask about EcoCharge Ghana.

How do I start charging my vehicle?
Open the EcoCharge app, find a nearby station, and either scan the QR code on the charger or select "Charge Now" if you're already at the station. Charging starts automatically once your wallet has sufficient balance.
How do I pay for charging?
EcoCharge uses an in-app wallet. Top up via card or mobile money, and each charging session is billed automatically based on the actual energy (kWh) delivered — no separate checkout step needed.
Can I reserve a charging bay in advance?
Yes. The app lets you reserve a specific charger for a future time slot, so you don't have to wait when you arrive. Reservations are held for a short grace period after your selected time.
What is the 20L clean water bundle?
Every charging session includes 20 litres of clean water, collectable at the station. It's part of our mission to pair Ghana's EV transition with wider community benefit, not just vehicle charging.
What connector types are supported?
Our stations support CCS2, CHAdeMO, and Type 2 (AC) connectors, with compatibility varying by station. The app shows which connector types are available at each location before you travel there.
Do you offer home charging?
EcoCharge Home+ lets subscribers link a compatible home charger to the app for remote start/stop, smart scheduling, and energy tracking — all from the same account used for public charging.
What if a charger isn't working?
Report it directly from the charger's detail screen in the app, or contact our support team. Our stations are monitored, and faulted chargers are flagged for maintenance.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. Payments are processed through Paystack, a PCI-DSS certified payment provider. EcoCharge never stores your full card details — see our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I get a refund?
Refund eligibility and timelines are outlined in our Terms of Service. In general, unused wallet balances and amounts charged in error are eligible for review — contact support with your transaction reference.
How do I contact EcoCharge Ghana?
Visit our Contact page for email, phone, and WhatsApp support options.
