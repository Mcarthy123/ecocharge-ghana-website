Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers about charging, reservations, payments, Home+, and route planning.

Charging
Where can I charge?
EcoCharge stations are live across multiple cities in Ghana, including Accra, Kumasi, Tema, Takoradi, Tamale, Sunyani, Cape Coast, and Ho. The app shows all stations and their live availability.
How do I find a station?
Open the app and use "Find Stations" or the map view to see nearby chargers, along with bay availability and estimated wait.
How do I start charging?
Scan the QR code on the charger, or select "Charge Now" from the station screen if you're already there. Charging starts once your wallet has sufficient balance.
How do I know whether a charger is available?
The app shows a live bay count (e.g. "4/6 available") for each station, based on current charger status.
Reservations
How do reservations work?
You can reserve a specific charger for a future time slot from the app. No payment is taken until your charging session actually begins.
What happens if I arrive late?
Reservations are held for a grace period after your selected time. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
What happens if I don't show up?
Your reservation may be released after the grace period so other users can charge. Since no payment is taken until a session starts, a no-show does not itself incur a charge under current policy.
Can I cancel a reservation?
Yes, from the "My Bookings" section of the app before your session starts.
Payments
How do I pay?
Top up your EcoCharge Wallet using card or mobile money via Paystack. Charging sessions are billed automatically from your wallet based on actual energy used.
What is the EcoCharge Wallet?
A prepaid in-app balance used to pay for charging and subscriptions. It has no cash value outside the app and can't be transferred to other users.
How does Paystack work?
Paystack is our payment processor. When you top up your wallet or pay for a subscription, Paystack securely handles the card/mobile money transaction. EcoCharge does not receive or store your full card details.
What happens if I am charged incorrectly?
Contact support with your transaction reference. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for how duplicate or incorrect charges are handled.
EcoCharge Home+
What is EcoCharge Home+?
A paid subscription tier for drivers with a home charger — it adds smart scheduling, battery protection settings, energy tracking, and remote charger control from the app.
What chargers are compatible?
Home+ currently supports chargers compatible with our OCPP-based charger network. Compatibility is confirmed during the charger linking process in the app.
How do I connect my home charger?
Use Quick Connect in the Home+ section of the app to scan your charger's QR code and securely link it to your account.
Can I schedule charging?
Yes — Home+ subscribers can set recurring charging schedules, including off-peak-only options.
Can family members use my charger?
You can invite family members into a shared Family group from the app. Shared wallet access and shared vehicle/charger use are not available yet — this is a planned feature.
Route Planning
Can EcoCharge plan short trips?
Yes. The AI Route Planner works for any distance — for local driving it acts as a driver assistant showing nearby stations and battery status; you don't need a long trip to use it.
Can it plan long-distance trips?
Yes. For longer journeys, it maps your route, estimates energy use, and recommends charging stops along the way based on your vehicle's range and current battery level.
How does it estimate battery range?
Using your vehicle's registered battery capacity and your manually entered current battery percentage. Live battery telemetry from the vehicle itself is not currently supported.
