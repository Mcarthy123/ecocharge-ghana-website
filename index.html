Our mission

Ghana's electric vehicle transition shouldn't come at the cost of energy access or clean water for the communities it passes through. EcoCharge Ghana was built on a simple idea: every charging station is also an opportunity to deliver something else people need. That's why every session includes a water bundle, and why every station we open creates local jobs and skills training rather than importing labor.

How it works

Drivers download the EcoCharge app, register their vehicle, and top up a digital wallet. From there, they can find nearby stations, reserve a charging bay ahead of time, or simply scan a QR code and start charging on arrival. Payment is deducted automatically from the wallet based on actual energy used — no cash handling, no surprises.

Where we operate

EcoCharge stations are live across multiple regions in Ghana, including Accra, Kumasi, Tema, Takoradi, Tamale, Sunyani, Cape Coast, and Ho — with new stations added as the network grows.
