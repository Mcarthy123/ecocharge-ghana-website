Our Mission

Driving the energy transition that uplifts communities.

Ghana's electric vehicle transition shouldn't come at the cost of energy access or clean water for the communities it passes through.

EcoCharge Ghana was built on a simple idea: every charging station is also an opportunity to deliver something else people need. That's why every session includes a water bundle, and why every station we open creates local jobs and skills training rather than importing labor.

The EcoCharge App

Everything you need to charge, in one app.

Find & Reserve

Find nearby stations, check real-time charger availability, and reserve a bay ahead of time.

Wallet & Payments

Top up your EcoCharge Wallet and pay automatically per kWh used — no cash, no separate checkout.

Vehicle Registry

Register your vehicle for accurate range estimates, connector matching, and charging history.

AI Route Planner

Plan short local trips or long-distance journeys — the app estimates your battery on arrival and recommends charging stops along your route based on your real battery level and vehicle range.

Charging History

Track every session — energy used, cost, and station — in one place.

EcoCharge Home+

Link a compatible home charger for remote start/stop, charging schedules, and energy tracking. Learn more ↓

Home Charging

EcoCharge Home+

A subscription-based smart charging dashboard for drivers with a compatible home charger.

Charger Linking

Link a compatible home charger to your account to monitor and control it from the app.

Smart Scheduling

Set automatic charging schedules, including off-peak-only options.

Battery Protection

Set a preferred daily charge limit (80/90/100%) to help manage long-term battery health.

Energy Tracking

Monitor monthly energy use and estimated electricity cost for your home charger.

Family Sharing

Invite family members into a shared group. (Shared wallet and vehicle access are not available yet.)

Charging Assistant

Rule-based recommendations using your real wallet, schedule, and vehicle data — not a live conversational AI.
