Privacy Policy

Last updated: [INSERT PUBLICATION DATE]

EcoCharge Ghana Ltd. ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the EcoCharge Ghana mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password.
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, connector type, battery capacity, and related specs you provide.
  • Charging history: session records, energy delivered, duration, and station used.
  • Reservation information: booking times, stations, and status.
  • Location information: approximate GPS location, used only with your permission to show nearby stations and support route planning while the app is in use.
  • Device/technical information: device type, operating system, app version, and identifiers used for security and analytics.
  • Payment/transaction information: wallet top-up history, subscription payments, and transaction references. We do not store your full card number — payments are processed by Paystack.
  • Home charger information (where applicable): linked charger identifiers, connection status, and usage data for EcoCharge Home+ subscribers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve the EcoCharge charging service
  • Process bookings, charging sessions, and payments
  • Manage your wallet, subscription, and transaction history
  • Send service notifications (sessions, wallet balance, bookings)
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations in the Republic of Ghana
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve app performance

3. How Information Is Stored & Secured

Data is stored using industry-standard cloud database infrastructure with encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. We take reasonable measures to protect your information; no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Payment Processors

Payments are processed through Paystack. Paystack's own privacy and security practices govern the handling of your payment card details, which EcoCharge does not receive or store in full.

5. Analytics & Cookies

The EcoCharge mobile application does not currently use third-party advertising trackers. See our Cookie Policy for details on this website.

6. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Transaction records are retained for a minimum period to comply with applicable Ghanaian financial record-keeping requirements. [DECISION REQUIRED: EXACT RETENTION PERIOD]

7. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Paystack — for payment processing
  • Our database/hosting provider — for secure data storage
  • Law enforcement — when legally required under Ghanaian law
  • Station operators — limited data required to facilitate your charging session

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for location access via your device settings
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at ecochargeghanaltd@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

EcoCharge is not intended for use by persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via the app or email.
