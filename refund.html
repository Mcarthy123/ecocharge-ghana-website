Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [INSERT PUBLICATION DATE]

EcoCharge Ghana is committed to ensuring you only pay for the energy and services you actually receive. This policy explains when and how refunds or cancellations are handled.

1. Reservation Cancellation

You may cancel a reservation before your scheduled time slot at no charge, since no payment is taken until a charging session begins. [DECISION REQUIRED: WHETHER A LATE-CANCELLATION FEE APPLIES, AND THE CANCELLATION WINDOW]

2. No-Shows

If you do not arrive within the applicable grace period after your reserved time, the reservation may be released for other users. [DECISION REQUIRED: NO-SHOW GRACE PERIOD] Since payment is only taken when charging begins, a no-show does not itself incur a charge unless a separate no-show fee is introduced and clearly disclosed at the time of booking.

3. Failed Charging Sessions

If a charging session fails to start after your wallet balance was checked or reserved (for example, due to a charger fault or connectivity issue), any reserved amount is released back to your wallet automatically within 24 hours. If this does not occur, contact support for a manual review.

4. Charger Unavailable After Reservation

If a station or charger you reserved becomes unavailable before your session starts due to maintenance, power outage, or technical fault, no charge is applied for that reservation.

5. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you are billed more than once for the same session or top-up, or believe you were charged incorrectly, contact support with your transaction reference. Verified duplicate or incorrect charges are refunded to your EcoCharge Wallet, or to your original payment method where the Wallet is not applicable.

6. Wallet Top-Up Refunds

Wallet top-ups are generally non-refundable once credited, as the balance remains available for future use within the app. If a top-up was made in error (e.g. duplicate charge or incorrect amount due to a system fault), you may request a refund within [DECISION REQUIRED: REFUND REQUEST WINDOW] of the transaction.

7. Subscription Refunds

EcoCharge Plus, Home+, and Fleet subscription fees are billed at the start of each billing period. [DECISION REQUIRED: SUBSCRIPTION REFUND POLICY — PRORATED, NON-REFUNDABLE, OR OTHER] You may cancel a subscription at any time; access continues until the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team with:

  • Your registered name and phone number
  • The transaction, booking, or session reference
  • A brief description of the issue

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within [DECISION REQUIRED: SUPPORT RESPONSE TIME].

9. Processing Times

Refund processing times depend on the method:

  • Wallet credit: typically within 24 hours of approval
  • Original payment method (via Paystack): timing depends on your bank or mobile money provider
